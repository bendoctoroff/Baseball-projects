
## Results
Validated against actual 2026 results for 289 hitters:
- Mean absolute error: 20.3 wRC+
- Mean error: -3.7 (small average bias)
- Most accurate for hitters between 80 and 120 wRC+

Misses were directional: the model undervalued young players with
elite physical tools and overvalued aging players with long track
records, consistent with public systems like ZiPS and Steamer.
